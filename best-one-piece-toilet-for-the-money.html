Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Elongated vs compact bowls: an elongated bowl is roughly two inches longer and more comfortable for most adults, while a compact bowl like the HOROW T0338W trades that length for a smaller footprint that fits tight bathrooms.
  • Same product, different price tiers: three of the picks below are Casta Diva's Elongated One Piece toilet sold at different configurations, from $225.70 up to $249.99, below the brand's $289.99 flagship. Comparing those tiers is exactly the kind of decision that determines the best one piece toilet for the money for your budget.
  • Measure your rough-in first: that is the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain bolts. The HOROW pick is built around a 12-inch rough-in, and you should confirm yours matches before ordering any of these.
  • Seats are usually separate: most listings in this price range do not include a seat, so budget another $30 to $50 unless the current listing says otherwise.
  • One-piece means fewer seams to clean: the tank and bowl are molded together, which cuts out the tank-to-bowl gap that traps grime on two-piece toilets, though the fixture is heavier to install as a result.
