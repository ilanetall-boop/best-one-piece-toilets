Things to Know Before You Buy

  • Rough-in size comes first. All seven toilets here use a standard 12-inch rough-in, the distance from the wall to the center of the floor drain bolts. Measure yours before you buy, because a 10-inch or 14-inch rough-in won't fit.
  • Comfort height is a specific measurement, not a marketing term. It means a seat 17 to 19 inches off the floor, matching ADA guidelines and standard chair height, and it's noticeably easier on your knees than the 14-to-15-inch bowls common in older homes.
  • MAP score predicts real flush performance. The Maximum Performance test measures how many grams of solid waste a toilet clears in one flush. Every model in this guide is rated at 1000 grams, the top tier most brands publish.
  • Soft-close seats protect more than your ears. They stop the lid from slamming, which is the biggest cause of cracked hinges and worn-out seats over time.
  • Skirted designs cost more but clean up faster. A seamless, skirted one-piece toilet has no exposed bolts or crevices at the base, so a quick wipe is usually all the cleaning it needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "one piece" mean on a toilet, and does it matter?
A one piece toilet molds the tank and bowl into a single unit instead of bolting two separate pieces together. That means no seam at the base where water and grime collect, which is the main reason people search for the best one piece toilet seat over a standard two-piece model. It also tends to look lower profile and modern, though it usually costs more than a comparable two-piece toilet.
What rough-in size do I need for these toilets?
All seven toilets in this guide use a standard 12-inch rough-in, meaning the distance from the finished wall to the center of the floor drain bolts measures 12 inches. Measure your existing toilet's rough-in before ordering, since a mismatch means the new toilet won't sit flush against the wall.
Is a higher MAP score worth paying more for?
Yes, within reason. The Maximum Performance test measures how many grams of solid waste a toilet clears in one flush, and every toilet in this guide scores 1000 grams, which is the top tier most manufacturers publish. You don't need to pay more than that for a higher number, since 1000 grams already covers real-world use without double-flushing.
