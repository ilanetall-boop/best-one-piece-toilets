Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Swiss Madison Ivy One Piece Toilet Dual a good replacement for an older two-piece toilet?
Yes, for most bathrooms. The Ivy uses a standard 12-inch rough-in, which matches what the vast majority of US homes already have plumbed, so installation does not usually require moving the waste line or modifying the floor. Because it is a one-piece design, you also skip the bolts and seam that collect grime between the tank and bowl on an older two-piece toilet.
Does the Ivy One Piece Toilet Dual come with a bidet?
No. The Ivy is a standard dual-flush one-piece toilet without bidet functions. If you want a built-in bidet, the Glendan smart toilet covered in this review includes one at a similar price, which is worth considering if wash-and-dry functions matter more to you than saving a few dollars on the Ivy.
How does the Ivy's price compare to similar one-piece toilets?
At $336.39, the Ivy falls between the Glendan smart toilet at $332.99 and the two taller one-piece picks at $339.99 each, making it one of the more affordable options in this comparison despite its dual-flush feature. The roughly seven-dollar spread across all four toilets means price alone will not decide which one is right for your bathroom, so the deciding factor should be which feature matters most to you: the standard flush, the bidet, or the extra height.
